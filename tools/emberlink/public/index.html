EmberLink

Share a message that can only be viewed once. Encrypted in your browser.

How to use
Write your message, optionally set a password and expiration, then click Create Link. The link contains the decryption key after the # symbol — keep it private.

Privacy Notice
We do not save any of your information. The message is encrypted in your browser before being sent.
After you close this page, the content cannot be retrieved from our side.
Once the maximum number of views is reached or the link expires, the encrypted data is permanently deleted from the server and cannot be recovered.

Limitations
• Images must be smaller than 500KB
• Message is encrypted in your browser before being sent
• After the maximum views are reached, the message is permanently deleted
• We have implemented technical measures to prevent preview bots (such as Slack, Discord, etc.) from accessing the content, but due to ongoing technological progress, we cannot guarantee 100% prevention.
