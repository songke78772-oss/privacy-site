Burn Chat

Temporary group chat. Messages disappear when the room ends.

100% Temporary
All messages are deleted when the room expires or is dissolved. Nothing is stored permanently.

How to use

Choose the number of people, duration, and your name, then click Create Room. Share the generated invite links with others. Each link can only be used once.

Privacy Notice

Messages are not stored permanently. When the room expires or is dissolved by the owner, all messages are permanently deleted from the server.

Limitations

  • Maximum 8 people per room
  • Room duration is limited to 24 hours
  • Once the room ends, messages cannot be recovered
  • Deep encrypt option encrypts messages in the browser before sending
